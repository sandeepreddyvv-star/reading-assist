Reading Assist settings

Sites where Reading Assist is off

None. Use “Turn off on this site” in the toolbar popup to add one.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Alt+Shift+R turns Reading Assist on or off.
    Alt+Shift+S starts or stops reading aloud.

    You can change these at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

    Reset

    Privacy

    Reading Assist runs entirely in your browser. It doesn’t collect, send or sell anything about you or the pages you read. Your choices are stored with Chrome’s own sync storage.
